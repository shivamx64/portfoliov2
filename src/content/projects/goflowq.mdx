## Problem framing

Many background job systems feel straightforward until failure cases start stacking up. GoFlowQ is where I test the mechanics that make a worker system reliable in the unhappy path: leases, retries, stuck job detection, and clear recovery rules.

## Design constraints

- Prefer understandable scheduling over opaque magic.
- Make retries explicit so operators can reason about load.
- Preserve enough metadata to explain why a job is still running, delayed, or dead-lettered.

## Current direction

I am using the project to compare storage-backed queues with broker-backed approaches and to sharpen intuition around worker isolation, scheduling fairness, and delivery guarantees.

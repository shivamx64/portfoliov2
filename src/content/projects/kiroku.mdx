## What makes it useful

Kiroku solves a small annoying problem: engineering notes get scattered. Build logs live in one place, architecture notes in another, and the useful detail disappears before the writeup happens.

I built it as a working notebook for project logs, postmortems, reading notes, and longer technical pieces. The goal is memory, not a perfect CMS.

## Product principle

The system has to stay light enough to use while I am still debugging. A rough note should be able to turn into a public article without being rewritten from zero.

## Why it belongs in the portfolio

It reflects how I work: build the thing, leave a trail, come back later, and make the useful parts readable.

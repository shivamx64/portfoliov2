## What makes it useful

Kiroku is less about publishing and more about maintaining continuity. It helps me keep implementation logs, reading notes, and project writeups close enough together that ideas do not get stranded in different tools.

## Product principle

The system should feel lightweight while still being structured enough that a rough note can evolve into a public article without being rewritten from scratch.

## Why it belongs in the portfolio

It reflects how I like to work: build the thing, write through the thing, and make the learning reusable.
